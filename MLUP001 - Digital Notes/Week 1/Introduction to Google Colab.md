#### What is Google Colab?
-  Cloud-based Python notebook by Google
-  Works like Jupyter — but in your browser
-  Code runs on Google's servers
-  Access your work anywhere - even on your phone
-  Requires only a Google account & internet

#### Why use Google Colab?
-  No setup required
-  Runs in the cloud
-  Auto-save to Google Drive
-  Easy collaboration
-  Free GPU/TPU
